# Galaxy Empire

A full-stack multiplayer remake of the Tap4Fun MMO strategy game (iOS/Android, 2011). Players manage planetary colonies, gather resources, research technologies, build fleets, and compete for galactic dominance.

## Language

**Player**:
A human user with one or more planetary colonies.
_Avoid_: User, account, member

**Planet**:
A planetary colony owned by a Player, identified by Galaxy:System:Slot coordinates, with building slots, resource production, and defenses.

**Building**:
A structure built on a Planet, placed in a fixed-position grid slot. Mines must be placed next to resource patches; storages, shipyard, research lab, and other buildings occupy any empty slot.

**Fleet**:
A group of ships sent from one Planet to another on a specific Mission.

**Fleet**:
A group of ships sent from one Planet to another on a specific Mission.

**Mission**:
A fleet action — Attack, Transport, Colonize, Spy (probe), Deploy (move between own planets), or Recycle (collect debris). Combat resolves instantly on arrival via automated rounds; result generates a combat report and debris field.

**Dark Matter**:
Premium currency earned through quests or purchased with real money. Used to expedite actions, buy Commanders/Officers, and premium items.
